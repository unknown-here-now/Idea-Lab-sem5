A remarkably simple tool to find the perfect locations for delivery hubs, clinics, and emergency centers. We combine your data with live maps to show you exactly what your city needs.

How it works

From raw data to actionable insights in four steps.

1. Provide Locations

Upload a spreadsheet of neighborhoods, or simply click on the interactive map to drop custom pins where you want to build.

2. Live Data Scan

The system scans OpenStreetMap in real-time to count residential buildings and existing hospitals around your locations.

3. Smart Routing

Our algorithm groups neighborhoods together and places fulfillment hubs exactly where they can serve the most people efficiently.

4. Get Insights

View beautiful coverage radiuses on the map and read a plain-English strategy report explaining the results.
